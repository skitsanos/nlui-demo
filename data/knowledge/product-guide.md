# Product Selection Guide

Tags: products, laptops, monitors, accessories, recommendations, comparison

## Choosing a laptop

For frequent travel, favor a weight below 1.6 kg and at least 12 hours of stated battery life. General office work is comfortable with 16 GB RAM and 512 GB storage. Software development and large analytical workloads benefit from 32 GB RAM. Creative work should prioritize display size, memory, and storage before cosmetic differences.

## Choosing a monitor

An IPS or OLED panel with 2560x1440 or higher resolution is a useful starting point for design work. Higher refresh rates primarily benefit gaming and motion-heavy content. For text-heavy office work, resolution, ergonomic positioning, and an appropriate screen size usually matter more than maximum refresh rate.

## Building a home-office set

A basic set combines a monitor, keyboard, pointing device, and any required dock or hub. Ask about desk size, device ports, accessibility needs, and budget before recommending multiple items. Availability and price must come from the current catalog data.

## Comparing products

Compare no more than four products at a time. Show the requested preferences first, followed by price, stock, rating, and the most relevant technical attributes. Do not claim that a higher-priced product is automatically better.

## Recommendation limits

Recommendations are catalog matches, not professional advice. The assistant may filter and rank available products but cannot invent specifications, discounts, warranties, or stock.
