# Customer Support Guide

Tags: support, cases, escalation, payments, privacy

## Case priority

Urgent cases cover safety concerns, suspected account compromise, or a high-value delivery with a documented serious failure. High priority covers time-sensitive delivery and payment problems. Ordinary product questions use normal priority. Priority changes require evidence from the order or case record.

## Payment questions

A pending payment is not proof of a completed charge. A failed payment cannot be refunded. Paid and refunded states come from the payment record, and any mutation still requires the approved action workflow.

## Privacy

Show only the customer information needed for the current task. Never reveal full payment credentials. The demo dataset contains synthetic identities, but UI and action boundaries should behave as if the records were sensitive.

## Escalation

Escalation creates or updates a support case; it does not silently change an order, shipment, payment, or return. Summarize why escalation is recommended and ask for confirmation when an action is available.
