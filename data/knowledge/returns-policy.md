# Returns and Refunds Policy

Tags: returns, refunds, exchanges, damaged items, eligibility

## Standard return window

Most products may be returned within 30 calendar days after the recorded delivery date. The product must include its serial-numbered parts, accessories, and original packaging. A return request is not a completed refund: it first receives a return authorization number and instructions.

## Non-returnable items

Opened software activation cards, personalized products, gift cards, and consumables with a broken hygiene seal are final sale. Products marked final sale on the order are also excluded. These rules come from the catalog and order record; the assistant cannot invent or override them.

## Damaged or incorrect items

Report a damaged, defective, or incorrect item as soon as possible. The support team may request photographs or serial numbers. Approved damage claims receive a prepaid return label and are not charged return shipping.

## Refund timing and amount

After the warehouse receives and inspects an approved return, the refund is sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. The refund covers the returned merchandise and its associated tax. Expedited shipping charges are not refundable unless the item was damaged or incorrect.

## Exchanges

The demo store handles exchanges as a return followed by a new order. Price differences and current stock are confirmed before either action is submitted.
