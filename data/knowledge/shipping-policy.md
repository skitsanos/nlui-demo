# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Tags: shipping, delivery, address changes, delays, tracking

## Delivery options

Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 6 business days after dispatch. Standard delivery is free when the discounted merchandise subtotal is at least EUR 100; smaller orders pay the rate shown at checkout. Expedited delivery, when offered, is priced separately.

## Address changes

A delivery address can be changed only while an order is still processing and before the carrier's first scan. The customer must review the complete replacement address and explicitly confirm the change. Support cannot redirect an in-transit or delivered shipment through this workflow.

## Tracking

Tracking details appear after a shipping label is created. A label-created status means the carrier may not yet have the parcel. Carrier events can take up to 12 hours to appear in the demo timeline.

## Delayed orders

An order is marked delayed when its current estimated delivery is later than the original service window. Check the order and tracking record before escalating. High-value orders above EUR 500 or urgent open support cases should be surfaced clearly, but escalation never cancels or refunds an order automatically.

## Lost packages

Contact support when tracking has not changed for 5 business days beyond the estimate. Support opens a carrier investigation before offering a replacement or refund.
